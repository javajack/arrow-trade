06Questions

The questions serious people actually ask.

What returns can I expect?
None are promised or represented, and that is deliberate. SEBI rules prohibit brokers from referencing past or expected algo performance, and most individual derivatives traders lose money. We sell governed access, execution quality, transparent cost and hard risk controls. Any firm leading with a return number is one to be careful with.
Is this portfolio management or a fund?
No. It is not PMS, not an AIF, and not pooled. Strategies run inside your own broking account. Your funds and securities remain in your name and custody, and you can pause or stop any strategy yourself, at any time.
Do I need to register my algorithms with SEBI?
No. Arrow registers each catalogue strategy with the exchanges and carries the compliance as the broker. You subscribe to strategies that are already approved and tagged. You never file a registration, run a static IP, or operate a server.
iRage runs proprietary trading. Will you trade against me?
No. Hard information barriers separate the proprietary desk from client operations. The desk has no access to client orders ahead of execution, conflicts are formally disclosed, and trading-ahead surveillance is active. We treat this as the most important promise we make.
What does it cost?
Pricing is built around access, governance and service: a membership, capacity-based strategy access, and brokerage, with every charge disclosed up front. There are no performance fees and no profit share, because we do not price against outcomes. The desk will walk you through the exact numbers during your consultation.
