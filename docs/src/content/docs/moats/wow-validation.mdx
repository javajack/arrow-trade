A feature is only a "wow" if real users say so without being paid to. This page audits every major wow claim in the strategy against observable evidence — app-store ratings, community behavior, referral statistics, embedded testimonials, press coverage of user anger — and is honest about which claims rest on strong proof versus directional proxies.

## Evidence grading

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <Badge text="STRONG" variant="success" /> | Quantified public behavior: referral percentages, rating volumes, community size, documented user migration |
| <Badge text="MODERATE" variant="caution" /> | Visible social signal: embedded testimonials, recurring community themes, press coverage of user sentiment |
| <Badge text="PROXY" variant="danger" /> | Inferred from incumbent investment or complaint patterns; needs primary validation before betting launch scope on it |

## Validated wow moments

### Money and trust

| Wow moment | Evidence | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekend instant withdrawal** ("money came on Sunday") | Zerodha is the only broker that publishes concrete terms — 9 AM–4 PM *all days including weekends*, ₹100–₹2L, free ([support doc](https://support.zerodha.com/category/funds/fund-withdrawal/withdrawal-process/articles/instant-fund-withdrawal)). A published, defended policy of this specificity is an investment signal: brokers don't maintain weekend payout rails unless users reward it. The bank-SMS screenshot is the natural shareable artifact | <Badge text="MODERATE" variant="caution" /> |
| **Trust-by-restraint compounds into referrals** | The single strongest number in the entire research corpus: Zerodha states 50% of new accounts came from referrals at peak, and ~30% continued *after monetary incentives stopped* ([Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/business-updates/zerodhas-referral-program-is-back)). Independently, ET reported Kamath attributing growth to trust and referrals, not incentives ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/news/customer-trust-and-referrals-drive-zerodha-not-incentives-nithin-kamath/articleshow/123389482.cms)) | <Badge text="STRONG" variant="success" /> |
| **Outage transparency matters** (inverse validation) | User rage is the proof: press coverage of Zerodha's September 2025 glitch and the December 2025 Cloudflare outage hitting Zerodha/Groww/Angel during market hours shows outages dominate trader perception ([TOI Sep 2025](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/zerodha-faces-outage-company-acknowledges-glitch/articleshow/123670091.cms), [TOI Dec 2025](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/zerodha-groww-and-other-major-stocker-broker-websites-hit-by-cloudflare-outage-go-offline/articleshow/125786127.cms)). Fyers and Dhan both invested in public status pages — competitive confirmation that reliability proof is demanded ([FYERS Status](https://status.fyers.in/), [Dhan Status](https://status-monitor.dhan.co/)) | <Badge text="STRONG" variant="success" /> |
| **No-spam / no-RM as a loved feature** | Dhan puts "No Calls · No Advice · No SMS · No RMs · No Spam · No Data Sharing" on its homepage *as marketing* and embeds X testimonials beside it ([Dhan](https://dhan.co/)); Kamath frames no-spam/no-tracking as part of Zerodha's moat in press ([Indiatimes](https://indiatimes.com/trending/zerodhas-nithin-kamath-explains-how-no-investor-pressure-shaped-long-term-decisions-netizens-say-patience-luck-keys-to-success-670465.html)). Two trust leaders independently converging on the same promise is strong directional proof | <Badge text="STRONG" variant="success" /> |

### Negative validation — what users punish

Word of mouth is asymmetric: anger spreads faster than delight. The recurring public punishment patterns, each of which Arrow's design must treat as a never-event:

| Punished experience | Public evidence |
|---|---|
| Outage silence during open positions | Press coverage + app-review anger after every major incident; Kite's 4.3 iOS rating vs Groww's 4.6 partly reflects accumulated outage memory ([brand research](/experience/support-trust-brand/)) |
| Margin surprises and unexplained RMS rejections | Persistent theme across trader forums and support taxonomies; the reason Zerodha built Nudge and Dhan exposes OMS error codes in its API |
| "Why was I charged?" moments | Every broker's pricing page now itemizes DP/square-off/gateway charges — an industry-wide scar from years of bill-shock complaints |
| Referral terms changed retroactively | Zerodha's own referral-restart post drew angry comments from excluded old referrers ([Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/business-updates/zerodhas-referral-program-is-back)) — trust features become trust liabilities when terms shift |
| iOS neglect for trader apps | Dhan: 4.6–4.7 on Android but 3.2 on iOS (1.4k reviews) ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/dhan-stock-market-trading-app/id1575318726)) — platform-quality asymmetry is visible and costs love |

## The word-of-mouth conversion model

Synthesizing all the evidence, wow moments convert into growth through exactly four loops — every launch feature should name which loop it feeds:

1. **The screenshot loop** — savings cards, payout SMS, latency timelines, dividend income, portfolio health. *Design requirement: every proof surface must be share-ready (clean crop, no sensitive data, implicit branding).*
2. **The rescue story** — "they warned me before the margin call", "withdrawal failed and it auto-retried", "support quoted my exact order ID." Rescue stories outperform feature praise because they're narratives, not specs.
3. **The artifact loop** — working API examples, shareable layouts, screeners, education chapters. Artifacts get forwarded; the brand rides along.
4. **The credibility transfer** — CA recommends the tax packet, developer recommends the API, trader recommends to family. Each segment's champion converts the next segment (see [Segment Wedges](/moats/segment-wedges/)).

## Open validation gaps and how to close them cheaply

Claims graded <Badge text="PROXY" variant="danger" /> above should be validated before they earn launch scope. None of these requires spend beyond design-partner effort:

| Claim to validate | Cheapest credible method |
|---|---|
| Expiry Mode is a switching trigger (not just a nice-to-have) | Design-partner cohort: instrument which Phase-0 beta features are used on expiry Thursdays vs other days; interview the 20 heaviest expiry traders |
| Latency Ledger changes broker choice | A/B the pitch in design-partner recruiting: "₹10 brokerage" vs "see where your order spent time" — measure which converts serious traders |
| Speed tools drive migration (vs retention only) | Mine app-store reviews and r/IndianStockMarket / r/IndianStreetBets posts mentioning *why* users switched brokers; classify stated reasons |
| True Cost Meter is shareable, not just useful | Ship a web-only cost calculator pre-launch (no account needed); measure organic shares and return visits |
| CA packet converts CA-recommended accounts | Pilot with 10 CAs in tax season; count client-book referrals attributable to the packet |
| Vernacular risk-first content builds tier-2/3 trust | Publish 5 Hindi explainers pre-launch; compare completion and share rates vs English equivalents |

<Aside type="tip" title="Standing validation infrastructure">
Make validation continuous rather than a one-time study: a monthly mining pass over app-store reviews (all top-10 brokers), broker-mention sentiment on X/Reddit, and competitor community threads (MadeForTrade, TradingQ&A) — classified into the wow/punishment taxonomy above. The competitors' users are publicly writing Arrow's requirements document every day; the only cost is reading it systematically.
</Aside>
