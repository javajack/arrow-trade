The targeting logic is **inverse profiling**: instead of defining a small-retail user and serving them, define everyone who is demonstrably *not* small retail and serve those. Four profiles qualify, and they share a pain surface that discount brokers and SaaS tools structurally cannot address. Evidence detail and sources: `research/17` in the repo.

## The four profiles

| Profile | Capital / behaviour | What they want | What they won't tolerate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HNI active trader** | ₹20L–1cr; option selling/buying, part discretionary, part API | Better execution, lower true cost, margin efficiency, clean tax | One bad incident, surprise charges, support silence |
| **UHNI / family office** | ₹1cr+; capital preservation + opportunistic F&O/commodity | Governance, capacity transparency, named service, conflict clarity | Black-box anything without controls, custody doubt |
| **Verified-PnL pro / supertrader** | High trade count; slippage/latency/cost sensitive | Deterministic execution, observability, speed, control | Fragile APIs, static-IP pain, silent order-semantics changes |
| **Small institution / treasury** | HUF/LLP/prop-like; audit & controls | Maker-checker, audit logs, policy limits, reporting | Consumer workflows, no institutional logs |

## The reframe: every pain is a premium-service surface

Discount brokers treat items 3, 8, 10, 13 and 14 as cost centers to minimize. For the big-ticket segment they are exactly what a premium relationship is *for*. Arrow Elite's product ([Algo Cloud](/elite/algo-cloud/)) and service model turn each pain into a paid, governed capability:

| Pain cluster | Elite response |
|---|---|
| Strategy access + registration + fragility (1,2,3,9) | Broker-registered Algo Cloud: turnkey, hosted, zero client registration |
| Execution + latency + slippage (4,5,12) | Broker-native low-latency execution with measured latency ledger and order audit |
| Capacity + risk + margin (6,7,8) | Capacity caps, collateral optimizer, portfolio risk cockpit, per-strategy/account kill switches |
| Trust + tax + service (10,11,13,14) | iRage-anchored custody trust, CA-ready reporting, unified cockpit, named market-hours desk |
