## The reimagined goal in one paragraph

Arrow Trade is **part of iRage** — iRageCapital, a domestic HFT and market-making firm ([validated here](/elite/regulatory-and-trust/)). Arrow Elite takes iRage's institutional algo capability and productizes it for serious money: a **broker-hosted "Algo Cloud"** of pre-built strategies that Arrow itself pre-registers and exchange-whitelists under the SEBI 2025 framework, so a big-ticket client runs institutional-grade automation **in their own NSE/BSE/MCX account with zero registration burden** — plus white-glove discretionary brokerage. We stop chasing the loss-making small-retail F&O crowd and instead win the people who already have ₹20 lakh to many crores and the sophistication to deploy it.

## Hard scope (do not drift)

| In scope | Explicitly out of scope |
|---|---|
| NSE, BSE, MCX only | GIFT City / IFSC; USD / global products |
| Client-owned accounts (client keeps custody & control) | PMS / AIF pooling or discretionary mandate |
| Broker-registered strategy catalogue + premium brokerage | Return claims, performance leaderboards, "tips" |
| Big-ticket / high-frequency individuals | Mass small-ticket retail acquisition |

## The compliant one-liner

> **Broker-registered automated strategies in your own Arrow account — iRage-informed execution and risk design, capacity caps, transparent charges, conflict disclosures and a named market-hours desk. No returns are promised or represented.**

That sentence is the whole product and its guardrails at once. The [2022 SEBI return-claims ban](/elite/regulatory-and-trust/) means we never sell what the strategies *make*; we sell governed access to institutional capability that big-ticket traders cannot build alone.
