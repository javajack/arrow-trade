# Investor-Facing Research, Analytics and Ecosystem Research: Indian Brokers

Date: 2026-06-11  
Scope: investor-facing research, analytics, product breadth, wealth tools, education, community and partner ecosystems for a new Indian stock broking platform. Primary wedge remains active F&O traders and API-first pros; secondary wedge is long-term investors. Coverage includes the major NSE active-client brokers and relevant wealth/ecosystem comparables: Zerodha, Groww, Angel One, Upstox, Dhan, Fyers, Kotak Securities, ICICI Direct, HDFC Sky, Paytm Money, 5paisa, Shoonya/Finvasia, m.Stock, SBI Securities, Motilal Oswal, IIFL, Sahi, INDmoney and ecosystem products such as Tickertape, smallcase, Sensibull, Streak and LearnApp.

## 8. Strategic recommendation

Do not copy Groww's beginner simplicity at the expense of Arrow's F&O/API wedge, and do not copy ICICI Direct's research breadth with bank-broker density. The strongest entrant position is:

**A professional trading broker with an investor-grade wealth and research layer.**

That means:

- For active F&O traders: keep the core promise around execution, options analytics, APIs, reliability, risk and tax.
- For long-term investors: add portfolio intelligence, direct MFs, ETFs, IPOs, bonds, dividends, benchmark comparison, stock SIPs, baskets and family aggregation.
- For ecosystem: open APIs, partner marketplace, public community and education should create a flywheel similar to Zerodha, but with more native portfolio analytics than Zerodha and less product clutter than full-service brokers.
